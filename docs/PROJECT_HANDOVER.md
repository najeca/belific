## 2. What the app does today

### Windows desktop (Electron around the Expo web export)
- Desktop 3.69.0 to 3.72.0: no red mark and no priority anywhere (the stored value is kept, nothing sorts by it); an Add button and the N key open a step by step add dialog (Enter next, Esc back, Tab stays inside, keys 1 to 3 pick answers, the task is saved on screen 1); Repeats is a row in the list switcher (groups Daily, Weekly and so on, they fold and stay folded, the pattern in words, Until dates); task cards have the phone's slab shadow in light mode; a right click, Shift+F10 or the Menu key on a card, an Anytime card or a bubble opens one menu (Done, Edit, Skip today for a repeat, Move, Delete, More).
- Three columns: the left pane (Brain Dump and lists), the centre (Tasks board of days, the Calendar in Day, Week and Month, or Today) and the Timebox (a 24 hour day grid).
- Today (3.67.0, Y): the Now card and the hours per label strip side by side, then the timeline of bubbles beside the Anytime cards and Done today. Anytime cards reorder by mouse drag or Alt+Up and Alt+Down, saved in the same synced row as the phone. Settings has Daily hours switches, and the label form has one too. The Timebox stays on the right.
- A task has a title, emoji, label, list, notes, steps, a length, reminders, a repeat, Smaller tasks and an optional Must do. One details window per card.
- Repeating tasks are series. A single day can be moved, retimed or skipped. Repeat until ends a series. This and following splits it.
- A Break is free time drawn as a gap, and counts in no total.
- A Shift is a long block with others inside it. They are drawn inside it, count once and move with it.
- A Plan button opens Plan my day in the morning, Wrap up from 14:00, or Plan week on Sunday evening and Monday morning. Every answer saves at once with its own Undo.
- Earlier holds unticked one off tasks from past days. Clear tools empty a day, week, list or label.
- Also: undo and redo, search, quick capture with phrases, quick reminders, Trash, Analytics, Quests and streaks, templates, calendar subscriptions, a calendar feed link (the function is not deployed), export, notifications, a tray icon, Light and Dark.
- An account is optional. Signed out, the app never uses the network. Google and Apple sign in use the system browser. A sync line beside the avatar always shows the state.

### iPhone (native Expo app)
- First launch shows a Sign in screen: Continue with Google, Continue with Apple, or Use without an account. An empty account asks before anything local is uploaded.
- Today has a Now card, an hours strip per label, a timeline, Anytime cards you can order by holding, an Earlier pill, and a Plan button that opens Plan my day before 14:00, Wrap up after 14:00, and Plan week on Sunday from 16:00 and Monday 04:00 to 13:59. A chevron beside it lists all three.
- Plan my day asks one task at a time: Today, Tomorrow, A time, Not today. Each answer has an Undo, and there is a summary.
- Wrap up asks each open task of today: Done, Tomorrow, Back to its list, Let go (a repeating day only Done and Let go for today). Plan week asks which day for last week's tasks, then Brain Dump and list tasks with no Day. Not now leaves a task as it is. Same Undo and summary style.
- Tasks shows every task as a card with its label colour. It has Lists, Labels (create, edit, delete), Repeats grouped in words, Search and Trash.
- Calendar has Day, Week and Month. Day draws shifts as shells and supports hold and drag in 15 minute steps. It can read the phone's own calendars, read only.
- The round + opens a step by step add flow. Press and hold opens a centred card of actions.
- The phone shows no priority and no red anywhere.

## 4. Decisions made and their reasons

- Phone only for this round, desktop unchanged. Reason: the desktop is the reference and must not move.
- Shared rules are reused, never copied (wrapActions, taskOps, nesting, timebox, taskSearch, trash). Reason: one rule set means the phone and desktop cannot drift apart.
- Red removed from the phone. Reason: the Motorways look and the plan's decision on quiet delete actions. The danger colour stays for the desktop.
- Sample events removed from Today and the Calendar. Reason: parity plan decision 1, they looked like real routines.
- Email sign in dropped, Google and Apple only. Reason: the shared Supabase email settings would change Landis and Proload too.
- Plan button on Today only. Reason: parity plan decision 3.
- Smaller tasks replaces Parts on both apps. Reason: parity plan decision 5.
- Not today on an unstarted repeating series refuses with a note, guarded in the phone path only. Reason: the shared skip would delete the whole series, and the shared code was not to change.
- Delete label on the phone calls the desktop's deleteLabel. Reason: same rule on both apps (tasks move to a same name label or lose the label, and the tasks stay).
- Now marker uses the accentText colour. Reason: the accent colour gave 4.41 to 1, and accentText gives at least 4.5 to 1 with the same look.
- Plan button follows the desktop's time rule (planFlowAt), with a chevron menu for the other two. Reason: same behaviour on both apps, and the user asked for the shared rules.
- Plan week asks only tasks with no Day plus last week's. Reason: a task already on a day moves from its own card. Wrap up skips quick reminders and rows inside a shift.
- Electron pinned exactly to 44.11.5, no npm audit fix force. Reason: stay inside major 44. The remaining desktop audit findings are build tools (electron-builder chain, undici, fast-uri), left alone.
- Terms sections 4 and 5 and the effective date (8 October 2026) were not changed. Reason: flagged for the user, not legal wording to edit unasked.
- No EAS build, no database change and no push this round. Reason: standing rule, Jethro does those.

- Desktop parity: the phone screens' rules moved to shared files before the desktop used them. Reason: one rule set, no second copy.
- Desktop Today is a new centre view with the Timebox beside it; the Tasks board today column does not follow the Anytime order yet; the Plan screens keep their one screen style. Reason: Jethro's answers of 2026-11-21.
- The Anytime drag on the desktop is the view's own pointer drag, not DragProvider. Reason: lower risk to the existing drags. An Anytime card cannot be dragged onto the Timebox from Today yet.
- The Now marker on the desktop Today uses the accent, not the red now token. Reason: no red, as on the phone.
- D2 to D5 choices (2026-11-22): the label colour stays the 6 px top band (the plan's 4 px left edge was not built, it would change every card check). The card slab is 6 px (the pane slab is 7). Delete actions keep their style (a quiet delete was not asked for). Repeats uses the remembered list setting. The data-missed hooks and lib/missed.ts keep their names. No inline New list or New label in the add dialog (a note points to the left pane). N is the add key. Priority sorting was removed in shared libs, so the phone's order changed too: this is a phone behaviour change that was not asked for, say if it should be restored.
- Deletion and export are by email for now. Reason: the in app Delete account function is not deployed.

# Session 2026-10-07, 2.12.3 (no layout shift on hover)

**Cause.** `TaskCard` mounted the calendar (move to day) and overflow icons only while the pointer was over the card, in the title row. They took width and height, so the title could re-wrap and the controls below moved a few pixels as the pointer arrived or left.

**Fix.** Both icons are always laid out at a fixed size (21 by 21) and only change opacity: transparent, inert (`pointerEvents none`) and out of the tab order when not hovered, visible and pressable on hover or while their popover is open. They are hidden while the title is being renamed. Nothing else changed.

**Test.** `npm run test:real:cp8e` (18 checks, isolated temp folder asserted, real mouse movement): for a short title, a title that wraps over several lines, a placed task, two left list tasks (one long) and an expanded card, every element inside the card (about 25 per card) is measured with the pointer away, over the card and away again, and none moves or resizes by even 0.1 px; the icons are transparent away and opaque on hover. The harness focuses the window and retries the move until the page has seen the pointer (an injected move is sometimes not turned into a hover). cp8b is stabler too: it focuses the window (a drag's dwell runs on animation frames, which only tick in a focused window) and waits for the highlight. cp8b (59 and 2), cp8c (24), cp8d (12), cp6 (61 and 60) and cp7 (19 and 2) pass.

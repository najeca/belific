# Session 2026-10-06, checkpoint 4 (drag and drop)

## What changed (2.8.0)
- Drag and drop across the three desktop panes, built on hand written pointer events (decision 019, Proposed). Pure maths in `mobile/lib/drag.ts` (snapping, clamping, drop resolution, past day rejection, week stepping, auto scroll speed, edge action). DOM side in `mobile/components/desktop/DragProvider.tsx` (one drag at a time, the lifted card, Escape, click swallowing, the auto scroll and dwell loop) and one hook, `usePaneScroll.ts`.
- Left row to a day column (Day set, no modal); left row to a Timebox slot (Timebox day plus the 30 minute slot under the pointer); card to another day; card or block to the left pane (Day and `startTime` cleared); block to a day column (Day set, `startTime` cleared); block move and bottom edge resize (30 minute steps, clamped to 06:00 to 23:00, minimum 30). Past days reject a drop. Escape cancels. A drop outside any target changes nothing.
- Edge dwell week change, wheel and edge auto scroll during a drag.
- Faint unlabelled half hour lines on the Timebox grid (the brief listed them as existing, but they were not in the code).
- The desktop panes moved from `mobile/app/components/desktop/` to `mobile/components/desktop/`, reached only through `DesktopEntry.web.tsx` (`DesktopEntry.tsx` renders nothing on iOS).

## The spike
Probed in the packaged app (`app://belific/`) with Playwright `page.mouse` (real input through Chromium's input pipeline, not DOM `.click()`):
- Pointer events: every `pointermove` reached a document listener with coordinates, a `wheel` arrived mid drag, and a `click` fired after `pointerup` (so a click after a drag must be swallowed; react-native-web's `onPress` comes from React's `onClick`, so a capture-phase `window` click listener does it).
- HTML5 drag (`draggable` set on the row): after `dragstart`, `pointermove` stopped (2 events in total) and no `wheel` event fired; there is also no way to resize with it. Pointer events were chosen. The HTML5 result was measured with Playwright's drag interception, not the physical Windows OS drag loop; the physical loop is known to swallow the wheel too, but that part is [unverified in this session].

## Why the isolation went further than the approved .native.tsx split
Every file under `mobile/app/` is an expo-router route, and the router bundles the whole folder. The iOS export before this checkpoint contained all six desktop panes as routes (`./components/desktop/KanbanPane.tsx` and so on, gap N8), whatever `app/index.tsx` imported. A platform split of `index.tsx` alone could not keep drag code out, so the desktop folder moved to `mobile/components/desktop/` and `index.tsx` imports `DesktopEntry`, which Metro resolves to `DesktopEntry.web.tsx` on web and `DesktopEntry.tsx` (returns null) on iOS.

## Choices where the spec was silent
1. Drag threshold 5 px; below that a press is a click and opens the editor.
2. Edge dwell: about 600 ms in a 48 px zone at the board's left or right edge; it repeats every 600 ms while held. The left edge never arms on the current week.
3. The board is narrower than a week (about 2.8 columns at 1440 px), so at an edge the board first scrolls sideways, and only at the end of its scroll does the dwell arm (with the quiet strip). Going forward lands on Monday, going back lands on Sunday.
4. A left row dropped on a slot keeps any existing duration; none means 30.
5. Drops on past days are rejected. The board never shows past days, so in practice this is the Timebox showing an earlier day (and a board left open past midnight). The cue: the lifted card shows a muted "Past day" and nothing is highlighted.
6. A card dropped on another day keeps its time (as "Move to day" does). A block dropped on a day loses its time (per the brief).
7. Rows and cards dropped on the Timebox use the slot under the pointer (10:47 goes to 10:30). A moved block keeps the point it was grabbed by and rounds to the nearest 30 minutes.
8. A card can also be dropped on a Timebox slot (the original checkpoint row had "kanban to Timebox hour"); same rule as a row.
9. Legacy Brain Dump items (from the phone) are draggable too; on a valid drop they convert into a Task (`convertDumpItem`) with the new Day and time.
10. Events (CustomEvents) are not draggable.
11. Resize keeps the start and never runs past 23:00. A block with a 15 or 45 minute duration from the chips snaps to whole 30 minute steps from its start on resize; a move puts its start on a 30 minute boundary.
12. Dropping something where it already is writes nothing.
13. Window blur and `pointercancel` cancel the drag, like Escape. Touch input is ignored on the desktop path.
14. Edge auto scroll: a 40 px zone at the top and bottom of each scrolling pane, up to 14 px per 60 Hz frame, scaled by real frame time.
15. Highlight styling: the target column gets the background colour and a 1 px accent outline; the left pane gets an accent border; the Timebox shows a dashed accent slot with "10:30 to 11:00"; the source is dimmed. The edge strip is the accent at 10% opacity with a chevron. No new colours; the shadow is the existing text colour at 14%.

## Bugs found and fixed
1. **Day columns registered without their clip** (found while wiring, before the run): React attaches child refs before parent refs, so a column registered before the board's node existed. The clip is now read lazily.
2. **Sideways auto scroll ran about three times too fast** (found by the real pointer run: 406 px in 150 ms on this machine): the per frame speed was tied to the display's refresh rate. Now scaled by elapsed time. An edge dwell drop in the new week then landed correctly.
3. **Test harness, not the app:** the first spike launch ignored an `APPDATA` override and opened the real desktop data folder. Nothing was dragged or edited (the probe timed out before any input) and the data files were unchanged (last written 2026-10-05), but the app's launch backup created `backups/2026-10-06`, a copy of unchanged data. Every later run used a wrapper that sets `userData` before `main.js` loads and asserts it.

## 2. What the app does today

### Windows desktop (Electron around the Expo web export)
- Three columns: the left pane (Brain Dump and lists), the centre (Tasks board of days, or the Calendar in Day, Week and Month) and the Timebox (a 24 hour day grid).
- A task has a title, emoji, label, list, notes, steps, a length, reminders, a repeat, Smaller tasks and an optional Must do. One details window per card.
- Repeating tasks are series. A single day can be moved, retimed or skipped. Repeat until ends a series. This and following splits it.
- A Break is free time drawn as a gap, and counts in no total.
- A Shift is a long block with others inside it. They are drawn inside it, count once and move with it.
- A Plan button opens Plan my day in the morning, Wrap up from 14:00, or Plan week on Sunday evening and Monday morning. Every answer saves at once with its own Undo.
- Earlier holds unticked one off tasks from past days. Clear tools empty a day, week, list or label.
- Also: undo and redo, search, quick capture with phrases, quick reminders, Trash, Analytics, Quests and streaks, templates, calendar subscriptions, a calendar feed link (the function is not deployed), export, notifications, a tray icon, Light and Dark.
- An account is optional. Signed out, the app never uses the network. Google and Apple sign in use the system browser. A sync line beside the avatar always shows the state.

### iPhone (native Expo app)
- First launch shows a Sign in screen: Continue with Google, Continue with Apple, or Use without an account. An empty account asks before anything local is uploaded.
- Today has a Now card, an hours strip per label, a timeline, Anytime cards you can order by holding, an Earlier pill, and a Plan button for Plan my day.
- Plan my day asks one task at a time: Today, Tomorrow, A time, Not today. Each answer has an Undo, and there is a summary.
- Tasks shows every task as a card with its label colour. It has Lists, Labels (create, edit, delete), Repeats grouped in words, Search and Trash.
- Calendar has Day, Week and Month. Day draws shifts as shells and supports hold and drag in 15 minute steps. It can read the phone's own calendars, read only.
- The round + opens a step by step add flow. Press and hold opens a centred card of actions.
- The phone shows no priority and no red anywhere.

## 4. Decisions made and their reasons

- Phone only for this round, desktop unchanged. Reason: the desktop is the reference and must not move.
- Shared rules are reused, never copied (wrapActions, taskOps, nesting, timebox, taskSearch, trash). Reason: one rule set means the phone and desktop cannot drift apart.
- Red removed from the phone. Reason: the Motorways look and the plan's decision on quiet delete actions. The danger colour stays for the desktop.
- Sample events removed from Today and the Calendar. Reason: parity plan decision 1, they looked like real routines.
- Email sign in dropped, Google and Apple only. Reason: the shared Supabase email settings would change Landis and Proload too.
- Plan button on Today only. Reason: parity plan decision 3.
- Smaller tasks replaces Parts on both apps. Reason: parity plan decision 5.
- Not today on an unstarted repeating series refuses with a note, guarded in the phone path only. Reason: the shared skip would delete the whole series, and the shared code was not to change.
- Delete label on the phone calls the desktop's deleteLabel. Reason: same rule on both apps (tasks move to a same name label or lose the label, and the tasks stay).
- Now marker uses the accentText colour. Reason: the accent colour gave 4.41 to 1, and accentText gives at least 4.5 to 1 with the same look.
- No EAS build, no database change and no push this round. Reason: standing rule, Jethro does those.

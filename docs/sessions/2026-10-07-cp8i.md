# Session 2026-10-07, 2.14.0 (checkpoint 8.4: 24 hour Timebox, repeating tasks as series, Actual time removed)

Three commits: **B and E** (remove Actual, spacing), **A and D** (24 hour Timebox, day start, overnight, column order), **C** (series with projected occurrences, docs, real check).

**B, Actual time removed.** The timer files, the Estimated and Actual row, the forgotten-timer prompt, `Task.actualSeconds`, the `actual_seconds` mapper column and capability flag (the 8.3 sync commit was reverted wholesale) and the two unapplied migration files are gone. `lib/legacyTask.ts` strips `actualSeconds` from a stored task when it is loaded. The chip and typed dropdown are untouched. Decision 022 is marked superseded.

**E, spacing.** Cards in a column 12px apart (`marginTop`), left rows 10px; the 2.12.3 hover icon space and cp8e still pass.

**A, 24 hour Timebox.** `GRID_START_HOUR 0`, `GRID_END_HOUR 24`; `clampStart` allows a start up to 23:30 whatever the length; `resizedDuration` up to 24h; `slotLabel` reads "22:00 to 06:00 next day". New `lib/dayStart.ts` (the 30 minute steps, default 05:00, where a day opens) and the main process setting `dayStart` (`desktop/src/settings.js`, validated; `DesktopSettings.dayStart`); the Settings modal has a Planning section with "My day starts at". The grid has spare room under 24:00 and 8px above 00:00 so any time can reach the top and the first label is not cut. `continuationOf` / `minutesPastMidnight` derive the "Continues from yesterday" block. Found and fixed on the way: the Daily reminder time input rejected every value (a pattern missing its backslashes).
**D, column order.** `kanban.ts` `compareItems`: untimed, then timed by start time, then completed; a card carried over from a past day counts as untimed.

**C, series.** `lib/series.ts` (pure: template, rule, projection, skip plan, end and delete plans, which stored rows an edit reaches), `lib/timeboxTasks.ts` (what a Timebox day draws, including a projected overnight routine), `lib/seriesActions.ts` (materialise on tick, patch the series, skip, end, delete: one serial chain over the normal storage calls), `taskOps.ts` (`opsForProjected`, series actions on repeating cards), `TaskCard` / `TaskBlockPopover` / `TaskPopovers` (projected styling, the quiet line, the series overflow menu), `KanbanPane` / `TimeboxPane` (project and draw), `DragProvider` (a projected card only lands on the Timebox and sets the series start time or estimate; no subtask merge for repeating or projected cards), `desktopNotifyCore` (projected occurrences in the 48 hour payload), `storage.ts` (keeps the tombstone of a skipped day while the day is today or later).

**Choices to know.**
- An unfinished past occurrence of a repeating task is no longer shown as overdue (it would sit next to today's projection for ever).
- Today's Timebox shows the rest of yesterday's overnight routine even when yesterday was only projected (display only).
- Edits on a projected card are written to the template row and every open stored row from today; completed rows other than the template are never touched, and the template keeps its Day and completed state.
- Skip tombstones keep the repeat rule and are the template of last resort only; Does not repeat and Delete repeating task clear the rule on every row, tombstones included.
- A repeating task with no Day still creates its next dated copy when completed on the desktop.
- Not covered: a stale device that ticks a projected day before syncing can recreate a row with the old rule.

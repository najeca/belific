### Behaviour changes for existing data (two old tests updated)
- Every 2 weeks **with days** used to be +14 days, ignoring the days (the old editor never offered days for it). Now the chosen days in alternate weeks. Example: a Monday task repeating on Fri now comes back that Friday, not in two weeks.
- Monthly completed late used to count a month from the completion date, so a 31 Aug task done on 5 Oct moved to 5 Nov. It now stays on its date number (31 Oct).
- Weekly with no days: still the Day's weekday. When done late, it is now the next of that weekday after today, rather than 7 days after today.
- iOS: no iPhone screen creates or edits task repeats, and the iPhone app does not call `setTaskCompleted` yet (checkpoint 5). Every stored kind still loads: `repeatKindOf` maps `weekly`, `biweekly`, `triweekly`, `daily` and `monthly`, and `nextOccurrence` handles all five. The iOS bundle gate passes.

### Choices where the brief was silent
1. Switching to Specific days or Every 2 weeks with nothing chosen preselects the weekday of the Day, if there is one; with no Day, nothing is preselected and Save waits for a day.
2. With no day chosen, the summary reads "Choose at least one day" (in the danger colour) and Save is disabled.
3. Weekdays and Weekends replace the selection (they do not add to it). They are text buttons, underlined when the selection matches.
4. Summaries: all seven days read "Repeats every day of the week"; Mon to Fri reads "every weekday (Mon to Fri)"; Sat and Sun reads "every weekend (Sat and Sun)". Monthly on the 29th to 31st adds "(the last day in shorter months)". Monthly with no Day adds ", counted from today (no Day set)" and uses today's date number.
5. The Day moves to the first chosen weekday on or after it when saving, not live in the Day field; the summary announces it first ("starts Thu 8 Oct"). A time on that task stays with it.
6. A stored monthly date number is kept while the Day still matches it after clamping (the 31st shown as 28 Feb stays 31). Editing the Day to another date takes the new date's number.
7. Every 2 weeks: the week of the task's Day is an "on" week. Completed in an off week, the next is the first chosen day of the following on week.
8. `triweekly` (never offered by any editor) shows as Every 2 weeks if it ever loads, and saving converts it; its next occurrence still works as every 3 weeks until then.
9. Short forms on rows use "Every 2 wks" to stay short.

## 3. Numbers
**Cause (measured in the packaged `app://belific/` build):** react-native-web gives every `<Text>` and `<TextInput>` the system stack `-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif` (Segoe UI on Windows). The page `body` has no font, so it computes to `"Times New Roman"`. The raw DOM controls used `font-family: inherit` and therefore rendered in Times New Roman: thin serif figures, e.g. the Day field showed "08/10/2026" in serif. These were the editor's Day `<input type="date">` and Custom `<select>`s, the board's "Jump to week" and "Move to date" inputs, and the Timebox date picker and event time inputs.

Inside React Native Text, numerals use the same Segoe UI as the letters (`font-variant-numeric: normal`, weight 400 or 700). The mismatch there was size and weight: the column count ("2") was 12px regular grey beside a 13px bold day title, duration chips were 11px regular, and Timebox hour labels were 11px regular grey. Those read thin in the screenshots. The week label ("Tue 6 Oct to Sun 11 Oct", 15px bold), "Scheduled 1h 30m" and the card time "09:30" already matched their letters and were left alone.

**Fix (desktop only, every place changed):**
- New `components/desktop/desktopFont.ts`: `DESKTOP_FONT_FAMILY`, the same stack react-native-web uses.
- `TaskModal.tsx`: `domInputStyle` (Day) and `domSelectStyle` (Hours, Minutes) use it instead of `inherit`.
- `KanbanPane.tsx`: `domTimeStyle` (Jump to week, Move to date) uses it. `count` goes from 12/400 to 13/600 (the day title's size and weight) with tabular figures. `chip` (durations) goes from 11/400 to 12/500.
- `TimeboxPane.tsx`: `domTimeStyle` (date picker, event start and end) uses it. `hourLabel` goes from 11/400 to 12/500 with tabular figures, so the hours line up; Segoe UI supports `tnum`.
- `BrainDumpPane.tsx`: `chip` (durations) goes from 11/400 to 12/500.
- The iPhone's typography is untouched (all desktop components, none imported by iOS).

**Before and after (screenshots of the Timebox, week board and editor, in the scratchpad):** the editor's Day field changed from serif "08/10/2026" to sans "06/10/2026" in the same face as the labels. Hour labels went from light 11px figures to slightly larger, medium-weight figures in a straight column. The column counts now sit at the same weight as "Today" or "Tomorrow" instead of looking like faint footnotes. The duration chips ("1h 30m", "15m", "2h") are readable at a glance and match the 12px label text beside them. The colours did not change.
